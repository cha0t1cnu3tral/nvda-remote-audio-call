Remote Audio and Call

Install on both computers, restart NVDA, and connect through Tools, Remote Access. Use exactly one controller and one controlled computer. No administrator access is required.

Call

On both computers, choose Tools, Remote Audio and Call, Choose microphone and speakers, select your devices and save. Then choose Start call. The other person chooses Answer or Decline. The incoming-call window also offers Microphone and speakers. Answer enables microphones. Use Mute microphone or Stop audio or hang up. Unanswered calls time out after 30 seconds. Use headphones to avoid echo. Device changes apply to the next call.

Computer audio

On the controlled computer choose Share computer audio (excluding NVDA). Applications play on the controller automatically. NVDA's process and child speech processes are excluded. Windows build 20348 or newer is required. Audio and Call are separate modes; switching ends the previous mode.

Settings

Choose your microphone, playback device and listening volume in Settings. Device changes apply to the next stream. Report status announces the current mode and whether microphone audio, remote audio or playback is missing. The Windows communications default may differ from your usual sound device. Assign optional shortcuts using NVDA Preferences, Input gestures, Remote Audio and Call.

Limits

This is a preview requiring NVDA 2025.1 or newer on Windows x64. It uses the existing Remote connection; some relays may filter extension messages. It has no acoustic echo cancellation or audio recording. Trust everyone with the Remote key and the relay operator. External speech applications outside NVDA's process tree may be captured.

Disconnecting, locking, device failure or exiting NVDA stops audio. It never restarts automatically. Secure desktop audio is unavailable.
